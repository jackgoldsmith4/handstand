# 100-Day Movement Plan

**Goal:** Loose, strong shoulders and open, controlled hips and legs — plus real bodyweight strength (push-ups, pull-ups, hangs, squats) and a few "yogi" party tricks (crow pose, bridge/wheel pose, pistol squat, standing splits) by Day 100.
**Equipment:** A pull-up bar (doorframe bar is fine), a wall, and floor space. Nothing else.
**Daily time:** 10–15 minutes, every day.
**Non-negotiable:** Every single day — training or rest — includes shoulder/posture work, hip mobility, and leg/hamstring flexibility.
**Starting point:** You can already do push-ups, pull-ups, and crow pose — the plan starts where you are and adds reps, sets, and hold time as the days go on, rather than starting from beginner regressions.

---

## Core Principles

1. **A small set of exercises, done consistently, beats a large set done once.** The entire program is built from twelve exercises. Nothing rotates in for variety's sake — the same movement just gets more reps or more seconds as the days go on.
2. **Mobility is trained daily; strength is trained in rotation.** Shoulders, hips, and legs get touched every day. Push, pull, legs, and skill work rotate through the week so each pattern gets real recovery between sessions.
3. **Only introduce a new movement where the old one genuinely can't be done yet.** Pistol squat and wheel pose are the exceptions — they use the same exercise slot across all 100 days, but the detail text underneath walks it from a regression to the real thing.
4. **Progress is measured in seconds and reps, retested on milestone days.** There are exactly three progression steps in the whole program — one per phase.
5. **Rest days are still mobility days.** Same three daily pillars, held gently, nothing else.

## The Twelve Exercises

| Exercise | Role |
|---|---|
| Doorway pec stretch | Daily shoulder / posture |
| 90/90 hip switch | Daily hip mobility |
| Standing forward fold | Daily leg / hamstring flexibility |
| Deep squat hold | Hip + ankle mobility, a resting position |
| Push-ups | Push strength |
| Pull-ups | Pull strength |
| Dead hang | Grip / shoulder, held for time |
| Pistol squat | Leg strength skill (regresses to bodyweight squat → assisted pistol early on) |
| Bridge pose (wheel) | Backbend / posture skill (regresses to glute bridge → single-leg glute bridge early on) |
| Crow pose | Arm-balance skill, held for time |
| Straddle stretch | Inner-thigh / hamstring flexibility |
| Standing split | Hamstring flexibility + balance |

Every one of these is reused many times across the 100 days. None are one-off.

## Weekly Rhythm

A repeating 7-day cycle — same weekday, same flavor of session, every week. Every training day includes the doorway pec stretch, the 90/90 hip switch, and the standing forward fold, plus that day's focus:

| Day of cycle | Focus | Exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Push | + Push-ups |
| 2 | Pull + hang time | + Pull-ups, Dead hang |
| 3 | Legs + backbend | + Pistol squat, Bridge pose (wheel) |
| 4 | Skill | + Crow pose |
| 5 | Full-body strength | + Push-ups, Pull-ups |
| 6 | Deep flexibility | Doorway pec stretch, 90/90 hip switch, + Deep squat hold, Straddle stretch, Standing split |
| 7 | Rest / recovery | Doorway pec stretch, 90/90 hip switch, Standing forward fold, Deep squat hold — all gentle, ~5 minutes |

Days 1–100 map onto this cycle continuously (Day 7, 14, 21 … 98 are recovery days), except for six milestone/test days that override the normal pattern — see below.

---

## Phases & Progression

Three phases, matching the app's phase indicator. Numbers step up once at each phase boundary — that's the entire progression system.

| Phase | Days | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–33 | **Open & Activate** — daily mobility baseline established; push-ups, pull-ups, dead hangs, and crow pose at a sustainable volume; pistol squat and wheel pose start at their regressions. |
| 2 | 34–67 | **Build Strength Through Range** — reps and hold times step up; pistol squat moves to assisted, wheel pose moves to single-leg bridge. |
| 3 | 68–100 | **Integrate & Flow** — heaviest volume and longest holds; pistol squat and wheel pose attempted for real. |

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shoulder / hip / leg stretch holds | 30s | 45s | 60s |
| Deep squat hold | 30s | 45s | 60s |
| Straddle / standing split holds | 40s | 55s | 75s |
| Dead hang | 3 × 20s | 3 × 35s | 3 × 50s |
| Push-ups | 3 × 12 | 3 × 16 | 3 × 20 |
| Pull-ups | 3 × 5 | 4 × 6 | 5 × 8 |
| Crow pose hold | 3 × 15s | 3 × 20s | 3 × 25s+ |
| Pistol squat | Bodyweight squat, 3 × 15 | Assisted pistol squat, 3 × 5/leg | Pistol squat, 3 × 5/leg, best effort |
| Bridge pose (wheel) | Glute bridge, 3 × 15 | Single-leg glute bridge, 3 × 8/leg | Wheel pose, 3 × best hold |

## Milestone / Test Days

Six days replace the normal rotation with a short battery of tests — no pass/fail, just a checkpoint you can compare against later:

| Day | Checkpoint |
|---|---|
| 15 | Foundation Check — deep squat hold, dead hang, pull-up max reps, push-up max reps |
| 33 | Phase 1 Complete — retest all four against Phase 2 targets |
| 50 | Midpoint Check — pull-up max reps, assisted pistol squat, crow pose hold |
| 67 | Phase 2 Complete — pull-up max reps, pistol squat attempt, crow pose hold, wheel pose attempt |
| 85 | Late Check — pull-up max reps, pistol squat best effort, standing split reach, crow hold |
| 100 | Capstone — everything at once: deep squat hold, dead hang, pull-ups, push-ups, pistol squat, crow pose, wheel pose, forward fold |

## Exercise Glossary (cues)

- **Doorway pec stretch** — forearm on a doorframe at shoulder height, step through until you feel a stretch across the chest and front of the shoulder.
- **90/90 hip switch** — sit with front leg bent 90° in front, back leg bent 90° to the side; rotate between both sides without using your hands.
- **Standing forward fold** — hinge at the hips, let the torso hang, knees soft, neck relaxed.
- **Deep squat hold** — feet flat, heels down, sit as low as your ankles allow, hold. If heels lift, place something thin under them until ankle mobility improves.
- **Dead hang** — full arm extension from the bar, shoulder blades relaxed then actively pulled down and back.
- **Pistol squat** — single-leg squat, other leg extended in front, arms forward for counterbalance. Early on, this slot is a plain bodyweight squat or a support-assisted single-leg squat instead.
- **Bridge pose (wheel)** — from your back, hands by your ears fingers pointing toward shoulders, feet flat near your hips, press up through hands and feet. Early on, this slot is a glute bridge instead.
- **Crow pose** — squat, hands flat on the floor shoulder-width apart, knees resting on the backs of the upper arms, lean weight forward off the feet.
- **Straddle stretch** — seated, legs wide, hinge forward from the hips with a flat back.
- **Standing split** — hinge forward from the hips, one leg lifts straight behind you as high as balance allows, hands can rest on the floor or blocks.
